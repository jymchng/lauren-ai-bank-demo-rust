# Fee Schedule

## Account Fees
- Monthly maintenance fee: $5 (waived with qualifying activities)
- Overdraft fee: $35 per occurrence
- Returned item fee: $35 per occurrence
- Stop payment fee: $25 per request

## Transfer Fees
- Domestic wire transfer: $25 outgoing, $15 incoming
- International wire transfer: $45 outgoing, $15 incoming
- ACH transfer: Free for all accounts

## ATM Fees
- In-network ATM: No fee
- Out-of-network ATM: $2.50 per transaction
- International ATM: $5.00 per transaction + 1% foreign transaction fee
