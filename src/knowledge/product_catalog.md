# Banking Product Catalog

## Savings Accounts
- **Basic Savings**: 0.5% APY, no minimum balance, $0 monthly fee
- **Premium Savings**: 1.2% APY, $10,000 minimum balance, $0 monthly fee
- **High-Yield Savings**: 2.0% APY, $50,000 minimum balance, $0 monthly fee

## Checking Accounts
- **Everyday Checking**: No minimum balance, $5 monthly fee (waived with direct deposit)
- **Premium Checking**: $1,000 minimum balance, $0 monthly fee, free checks
- **Student Checking**: No minimum balance, $0 monthly fee, age 13-24

## Certificates of Deposit (CDs)
- **6-Month CD**: 3.0% APY, $1,000 minimum
- **12-Month CD**: 3.5% APY, $1,000 minimum
- **24-Month CD**: 4.0% APY, $1,000 minimum
