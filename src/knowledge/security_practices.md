# Security Practices

## Account Security
- Use strong, unique passwords for your online banking
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on your account
- Never share your PIN or password with anyone
- Review your account statements regularly

## Online Banking Safety
- Always log out when finished with online banking
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking transactions
- Keep your browser and operating system up to date
- Be cautious of phishing emails and suspicious links

## Fraud Prevention
- Report lost or stolen cards immediately
- Monitor your credit report annually
- Set up transaction alerts on your account
- Contact us immediately if you notice unauthorized transactions
