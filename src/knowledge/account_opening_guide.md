# Account Opening Guide

## What You Need to Open an Account
- Valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport)
- Social Security Number
- Proof of address (utility bill, lease agreement)
- Initial deposit amount

## Account Types Available
- Personal checking and savings accounts
- Business accounts
- Student accounts (ages 13-24)
- Joint accounts

## Online Account Opening
1. Visit our website and click "Open an Account"
2. Select your preferred account type
3. Fill in your personal information
4. Verify your identity
5. Make your initial deposit
6. Start banking!

## In-Branch Account Opening
Visit any of our branches during business hours with the required documents. Our staff will guide you through the process.
