# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: How do I open an account?**
A: You can open an account online or visit any of our branch locations. You'll need a valid ID and initial deposit.

**Q: What are your business hours?**
A: Branches are open Monday-Friday 9AM-5PM and Saturday 9AM-1PM. Online banking is available 24/7.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, or contact customer support.

## Accounts

**Q: How do I check my balance?**
A: You can check your balance through our app, website, ATM, or by calling customer service.

**Q: How do I set up direct deposit?**
A: Provide your employer with your account number and routing number, available in your account settings.

**Q: What is the minimum balance?**
A: Standard accounts require a $100 minimum daily balance to avoid fees. Premium accounts have no minimum.

## Transfers

**Q: How long do transfers take?**
A: Internal transfers are instant. External transfers typically take 1-2 business days.

**Q: Can I cancel a transfer?**
A: Pending transfers can be cancelled. Completed transfers require a reversal request.

## Security

**Q: What should I do if I suspect fraud?**
A: Contact us immediately. Call 1-800-BANK-HELP or use the fraud reporting feature in the app.
