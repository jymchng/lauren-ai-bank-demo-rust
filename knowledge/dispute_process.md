# Dispute Process

## How to Dispute a Transaction

1. **Identify the transaction** in your account history
2. **Contact us** within 60 days of the transaction date
3. **Provide details** about why you believe the charge is incorrect
4. **Investigation** begins within 10 business days
5. **Resolution** typically within 45 days

## Types of Disputes

- **Unauthorized charges**: Transactions you did not make
- **Incorrect amount**: Charged a different amount than expected
- **Duplicate charges**: Same transaction charged twice
- **Services not received**: Paid for goods/services not delivered
- **Quality issues**: Goods/services significantly different from description

## Provisional Credit

While your dispute is being investigated, you may receive a provisional credit
for the disputed amount. This credit will be made permanent if the dispute is
resolved in your favor.

## Required Information

- Transaction date and amount
- Merchant name
- Reason for dispute
- Any supporting documentation
