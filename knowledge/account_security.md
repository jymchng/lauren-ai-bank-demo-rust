# Account Security

## Protecting Your Account

Your account security is our top priority. Here are some important tips:

- **Never share your password** with anyone, including bank employees.
- **Enable two-factor authentication** (2FA) for additional security.
- **Monitor your account** regularly for unauthorized transactions.
- **Report suspicious activity** immediately to our fraud department.

## Password Requirements

- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Cannot reuse the last 5 passwords
- Must be changed every 90 days

## Account Lockout

After 5 failed login attempts, your account will be locked for 30 minutes.
Contact customer support if you need immediate access.
