# Transfer Limits

## Daily Transfer Limits

| Account Type | Daily Limit | Per Transaction |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Standard    | $5,000      | $2,500          |
| Premium     | $25,000     | $10,000         |
| Business    | $100,000    | $50,000         |

## Transfer Processing Times

- **Internal transfers** (between your accounts): Instant
- **Domestic transfers**: 1-2 business days
- **International transfers**: 3-5 business days

## Transfer Requirements

- Both accounts must be verified
- Transfers over $1,000 require additional authentication
- International transfers may require SWIFT codes
