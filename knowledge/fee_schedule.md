# Fee Schedule

## Account Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| Monthly maintenance | $0 (with direct deposit) |
| Monthly maintenance | $12.99 (without direct deposit) |
| Overdraft | $35 per occurrence |
| Wire transfer (domestic) | $25 |
| Wire transfer (international) | $45 |
| Stop payment | $30 |

## ATM Fees

- **In-network ATM**: No fee
- **Out-of-network ATM**: $2.50 + ATM owner's fee
- **International ATM**: $5.00 + ATM owner's fee

## Transfer Fees

- **Internal transfer**: Free
- **External transfer (standard)**: $3.00
- **External transfer (express)**: $10.00
- **Peer-to-peer transfer**: Free

## Fee Waivers

Premium account holders receive fee waivers on:
- Monthly maintenance
- Out-of-network ATM fees (up to 4/month)
- Wire transfers (domestic)
